# Signal Wall

A static CRT-centered channel-wall prototype for evaluating local tuning, curation, and privacy boundaries. It contains fictional fixture channels only.

## Current features
- Local channel selection, hybrid URL state, power/mute, roving focus, effects intensity, and unavailable-route handling.
- No accounts, media, API, analytics, persistence, or third-party traffic.

## Future gates
External media, YouTube, AT Protocol, profiles, and watch rooms require separate rights, privacy, security, and architecture approval.
